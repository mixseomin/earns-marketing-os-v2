BAH comes down to three things: where you're stationed, your pay grade, and whether you have dependents.

1. Location. The rate is tied to the ZIP code of your permanent duty station, not where you choose to live. Live 40 minutes away in a cheaper town and you still get the duty station rate.

2. Pay grade. An E-4 and an O-3 at the same base get different amounts, because the rate is built from the cost of housing someone at that grade would typically rent.

3. Dependents. "With dependents" pays more than "without". It doesn't matter if you have one dependent or five, the rate is the same.

Where the numbers come from: every year DoD surveys local rents and utility costs in each housing area and sets new rates that start January 1. Members cover a small share of housing cost themselves, so BAH isn't meant to pay 100% of every rental.

Two things that surprise people:
- If the rate for your area drops, you keep your old, higher rate as long as you stay at the same duty station. That is called rate protection.
- A PCS resets it. New station, new ZIP, new rate, sometimes lower.

Quick self-check before you sign a lease:
- Which ZIP is my duty station, not my house?
- Am I listed with or without dependents?
- Does the rent plus utilities fit under the rate, with some room?

You can look up the exact monthly figure for any base, grade and dependent status here: https://militarycalc.com/bah?utm_source=quora&utm_medium=answer&utm_campaign=quora-02-bah-calculated

If your LES shows a different amount than the table, the usual cause is the wrong duty ZIP or dependent status on file. Your finance office can fix both.
