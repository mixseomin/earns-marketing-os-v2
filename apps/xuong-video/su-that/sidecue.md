## Product
- Sidecue is a teleprompter app for Windows 10 and Windows 11, sold on the Microsoft Store.
- Price: 7-day free trial, then US$7.99 paid once (no subscription).
- It shows the script in a slim window right under the webcam, on top of Zoom, Teams, Meet and slides, so the reader keeps eye contact.
- Windows leaves the Sidecue window out of screen sharing and recordings: the audience does not see it.
- The current line stays on a yellow band so a quick glance finds the place again; speed is set in words per minute.
- Phone as remote: scan a QR code, the phone browser shows the same lines scrolling in step, pause or jump back without the keyboard; nothing to install on the phone.
- Script cues: [PAUSE] markers and **bold** emphasis inside the script.
- Presentation clicker or foot pedal: Page Up / Page Down drive the script even while Zoom has focus.
- Teleprompter glass: mirror or flip the text, F11 for full screen on the rig monitor.
- Scripts are saved as text files in Documents\Sidecue; it opens Word, .txt and .md files.
- The app and the phone remote come in 12 languages.
