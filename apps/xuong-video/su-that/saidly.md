## Product
- Saidly is a transcription app for Windows 10 and Windows 11, sold on the Microsoft Store.
- Price: 7-day free trial with everything on, then US$14.99 paid once. No subscription, no account, no minute limits, no ads, no review pop-ups.
- Transcription runs on the user's own PC with an open speech model (Whisper). The model downloads once on first use; after that it works offline. Files are not sent to a server.
- Recognizes about 100 spoken languages.
- Speed example from the maker's test PC: about 80 seconds to transcribe a 15-minute recording in Fast mode. Three speeds: Fast, Balanced, Most accurate.
- Long files are fine (e.g. a two-hour lecture): lines appear as they are done, a bar shows time left, Cancel keeps what is already typed. Long files go in one-minute pieces.
- Can record a meeting: microphone, computer sound (Zoom, Teams, Meet, YouTube) or both; the audio is kept as a WAV next to the transcript.
- Exports: SRT or VTT subtitles (YouTube, Premiere, DaVinci) and plain TXT with or without timestamps.
- Click any line to fix a word; the fix carries into every export.
- Transcripts are saved in Documents\Saidly. Right-click an audio or video file and choose Open with Saidly. Re-runs old MP3, M4A or MP4 files.
- Accuracy: on clear speech close to typing it yourself; crosstalk, heavy accents and bad microphones lower it.
